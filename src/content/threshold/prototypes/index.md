Threshold is a multi-genre narrative anthology that explores the emotional complexity of becoming a father, built in Godot 4 (GDScript) for the browser first, then phone, then PC and Steam. Unlike traditional parenting simulators that focus on the mechanics of childcare, it focuses on the internal psychology of the father: the anxiety of providing, the instinct to protect, and the struggle to connect. A genre-as-metaphor approach presents distinct emotional challenges as unique gameplay experiences, and a House joins the short chapters and changes with the story.

Dan used **Cursor** and **Claude Code** as tools for the blueprints, and **Matt Pocock's skills** and **BMAD** as agent tools. The source of truth is the [public GitHub repository](https://github.com/dsickles/Threshold).
